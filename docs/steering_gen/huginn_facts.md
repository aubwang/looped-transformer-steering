## 2. respond_digits / respond_truth
Finding / accepted wording: - The local linear-response model predicted small-dose persistent responses (Spearman 0.995 digits, 0.999 truth). - Per unit push norm, immediate readout coupling was about 5× larger for the tested digit chords (0.207) than for the   mass-mean truth directions (0.041 CITIES, 0.038 COMPARE). This is mainly because the digit readout is more sensitive   (‖g‖ 2.76 vs 0.66); both kinds of direction are nearly orthogonal to their readout gradients (cos 0.08 vs 0.06). - The pooled unnormalized ratio (4.4×) is unstable: truth coupling splits into two dataset clusters (because of the   dose difference in §3.2), and its bootstrap CI is 3.0–40.7. - Stronger recurrent attenuation of the truth directions was not supported (ratio 0.62, CI −0.22 to 1.22). Among   positive responders, truth retention was somewhat lower than digit retention (geometric mean 0.63 vs 0.82), but   coupling accounts for 2.88 of the 3.14 log-unit gap. All-item retention medians (0.66 vs 0.41) mix in sign reversals. - These results favor weak immediate coupling over stronger recurrent attenuation as the explanation for the weak truth   steering. - Recurrence reversed the predicted sign of response on 42% of digit items and 19% of truth items (all in CITIES). - Digit responses were *superlinear* at full dose: median observed 3.72 versus a linear prediction of 1.44, and 20/21   small-dose reversals were positive at full dose. The +3.7 in §3.1 is therefore outside the regime where the linear   model is validated. The small-dose fit holds within each truth dataset. (`huginn_addition/WRITEUP.md:224–238`).
